## 10. Risk Factors & Mitigation

### 10.1 Financial Risks

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| Slow customer adoption | Medium | High | Freemium tier, partnership with consultants |
| Higher CAC than projected | Medium | Medium | Focus on content marketing, partnerships |
| Longer sales cycles (Enterprise) | High | Medium | Build SMB base first, land-and-expand |
| Delayed grant funding | Medium | Low | Conservative base case excludes grants |
| Currency fluctuation (if int'l) | Low | Low | EUR-based pricing for EU market |

### 10.2 Mitigation Strategies

1. **Revenue Diversification:** Multiple pricing tiers and revenue streams
2. **Cost Flexibility:** Variable cost structure, contractor-based scaling
3. **Cash Reserve:** Maintain 6-month runway minimum
4. **Grant Pipeline:** Pursue multiple funding programs simultaneously
5. **Early Monetization:** Focus on paying customers from Month 2
